Milestones That Define Us

From our first Formula Bharat entry to building central India's first electric race car — every achievement tells a story.

Competition
2024

Formula Bharat — AIR 8 Overall

Formula Ashwariders, our combustion team, secured an overall All India Rank of 8 at Formula Bharat 2024, its best national result to date.

India AIR 8
Competition
2024

Formula Bharat — AIR 19 (Electric)

E-Formula Ashwariders finished AIR 19 overall out of 45 electric teams nationwide, clearing technical inspection with an in-house manufactured accumulator container.

India AIR 19
Competition
2023

Formula Bharat — First On-Site Podium Push

In its first on-site competition, E-Formula Ashwa Riders finished 10th out of 37 electric teams at Kari Motor Speedway, Coimbatore.

Coimbatore, India AIR 10
Competition
2023

Formula Bharat — Combustion Category

Formula Ashwa Riders, our combustion team, finished 14th out of 49 registered teams at Formula Bharat 2023, held at Kari Motor Speedway.

Coimbatore, India AIR 14
Competition
2022

Formula Bharat (Virtual) — Vehicle ZEUS

In the virtual edition of Formula Bharat, the team competed with vehicle ZEUS, finishing 11th of 13 teams overall and reaching the semi-finals of the Endurance Event.

Virtual Event AIR 11
Award
2023

MathWorks Modelling Award

E-Formula Ashwa Riders ranked 5th nationally out of 86 registered teams in the MathWorks Modelling special award category at Formula Bharat 2023.

India Rank 5 of 86
Award
2023

Featured in Autocar India

The team's electric Formula Student journey was featured in Autocar India's March 2023 issue, covering the debut of central India's first electric race car.

India Media Feature
Award
2023

Engineering Design — 10th of 37

E-Formula Ashwa Riders placed 10th nationally in the Engineering Design Presentation at Formula Bharat 2023, competing against 37 electric teams.

India 10th of 37

The Beginning
