Engineering Systems

Built for Performance

Every vehicle subsystem is engineered to operate in total harmony at race speed.

Carbon Monocoque Chassis

Full carbon fiber monocoque designed for maximum structural rigidity and driver safety conforming to FIA Formula Student standards.

  • MaterialCarbon Fiber / Honeycomb
  • Weight28 kg
  • Torsional Rigidity45,000 Nm/deg
  • Safety StandardFIA Formula Student

Chassis Dimensions

Optimized geometry providing ideal weight distribution and driver ergonomics at maximum cornering loads.

  • Wheelbase1,550 mm
  • Front Track1,200 mm
  • Rear Track1,180 mm
  • CoG Height265 mm

Suspension Architecture

Double wishbone push-rod suspension at all four corners delivers precise handling and adjustable setup for varying circuit conditions.

  • TypeDouble Wishbone Push-Rod
  • DampersÖhlins TTX 2-way
  • Anti-roll BarsAdjustable Front & Rear
  • Wheel Travel60 mm

Braking System

Carbon-ceramic brake discs with regenerative braking blended to maximize energy recovery under deceleration.

  • Front DiscsCarbon-Ceramic 260mm
  • Calipers4-Piston Brembo
  • Regen BrakingUp to 80 kW recovery
  • Brake BiasAdjustable 40–60%

Aerodynamics Package

Full CFD-optimized aerodynamic suite delivering exceptional downforce-to-drag ratios for both high-speed stability and cornering grip.

  • Downforce @ 100 km/h450 kg
  • Drag Coefficient (Cd)0.68
  • Front Wing3-Element Carbon
  • Rear Wing2-Element + Active DRS

Underbody & Diffuser

Ground-effect underbody channels airflow through a full-width rear diffuser generating significant venturi suction at speed.

  • UnderbodyCarbon Ground Effect
  • DiffuserFull-Width 9-Channel
  • DRS Range0–12° Active Adjustment
  • SidepodsUndercut Carbon Composite

Vehicle Control Unit

Motec M150 ECU running proprietary real-time vehicle control software, power management, and safety interlocks.

  • ECUMotec M150
  • Sensors40+ Active Channels
  • Telemetry4G / Wi-Fi Real-time
  • Data Logging100 Hz Frequency

Battery System

600V high-voltage lithium-ion battery pack with active thermal management and multi-layer BMS protection.

  • Pack Voltage600 V nominal
  • Capacity7.2 kWh usable
  • Thermal Mgmt.Active liquid cooling
  • BMSMulti-layer protection

AI & Predictive Performance

Machine learning models running on-board analyze lap telematics in real-time for thermal optimization, traction control, and race strategy simulation.

  • Telemetry AIReal-time lap analytics
  • Predictive ModelThermal & energy forecasting
  • Driver AssistDynamic traction slip control
  • Race StrategyAutomated stint simulations
  • Vision SystemCamera-based track mapping
  • Compute PlatformNVIDIA Jetson embedded AI
From Concept to Circuit

The Build Journey

Ashwa-3 was designed, fabricated, and tested entirely by our student engineering team over 14 months.

01 // Phase

Concept & Design

Defining aerodynamic goals, suspension geometry, and electric powertrain layout using simulation tools.

Sep 2024 — Nov 2024
02 // Phase

CAD & Simulation

Full 3D modeling in CATIA V5 with CFD aero analysis and structural FEA stress testing.

Nov 2024 — Jan 2025
03 // Phase

Fabrication

Carbon fiber monocoque layup, CNC machined uprights, and welded sub-frame assembly completed.

Jan 2025 — Apr 2025
04 // Phase

Electronics Integration

600V battery pack installation, wiring harness routing, ECU mapping and sensor calibration.

Apr 2025 — Jun 2025
05 // Phase

Testing & Tuning

Skid-pad, autocross, and endurance testing with real-time telemetry data to refine setup.

Jun 2025 — Aug 2025
06 // Phase

Race Day

Full competition events including acceleration, skid-pad, autocross, and 22km endurance run.
